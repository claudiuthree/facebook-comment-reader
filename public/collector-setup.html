Set up the Windows Chrome collector

The Chrome collector opens a Facebook post in your signed-in desktop browser, copies visible comment text into the reader, and closes the tab when collection ends. It reads no passwords or commenter names.

  1. Download the collector ZIP and extract it to a folder on your PC.
  2. In Windows Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the extracted folder that contains manifest.json.
  4. Return to the comment reader and refresh it. You should see Chrome collector connected.
  5. Paste any public Facebook post link and choose Use this post. The new Facebook tab will close after collection. Press Read continuously when the first comments arrive.

For phone playback, choose Send this queue to my phone in the reader and open the link it shows on your phone. That link expires after 24 hours. Facebook may require a sign-in or may change its comment layout, so some posts or replies may remain unavailable.
