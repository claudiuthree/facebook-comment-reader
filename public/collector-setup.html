Set up the Windows Chrome mini player

This mini player reads visible comments directly on a Facebook post. It shows the loaded count and playback controls, and can copy the comment queue for phone playback. It does not read passwords or commenter names.

If you already installed the mini player, replace the files in its extracted folder, open chrome://extensions, and select Reload. Refresh any Facebook post tabs already open.

  1. Download the smaller Chrome extension ZIP and extract it to a folder on your PC. If Chrome or Defender blocks this version, stop and report the warning; do not restore the blocked file.
  2. In Windows Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the extracted folder that contains manifest.json.
  4. Open a Facebook post, click the extension icon, and press Collect and play. The extension selects Newest before collecting. Drag the mini player’s top bar to move it and its lower right corner to resize it.
  5. To listen on your phone, press Copy comments for phone. On the reader, select the same post link, paste the copied queue into Add copied comments, and press Add to listening queue. Then press Send this queue to my phone.

The mini player does not automatically open or close Facebook tabs from a link pasted into the reader. Phone links expire after 24 hours. Facebook may require a sign-in or may change its comment layout, so some posts or replies may remain unavailable.

Collection stops after 8 seconds without new comments or after 40 seconds overall. Stop stops collection and playback immediately. Clear collection also empties the loaded queue so you can start fresh.
